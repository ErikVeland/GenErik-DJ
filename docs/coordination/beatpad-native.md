# R135 original Reloop Beatpad

GenErik DJ opens the original Beatpad through Core MIDI when iPadOS exposes the USB device. The app pairs MIDI input and feedback on the same device. A wired Beatpad audio route selects master channels 1–2 and headphone channels 3–4. Keep the generic MIDI settings at their current values. [Reloop Beatpad](https://www.reloop.com/reloop-beatpad) · [Original MIDI map](https://www.reloop.com/media/custom/upload/Reloop-Reloop_Beatpad_MidiMap_v1.2.pdf)

The mapping covers Play, Sync, Set and Jump Cue, pitch, platter scratch, rim nudge, jog search, mixer controls, browse, load, loops, Hot Cues and sample pads. Shift+Load toggles fader start. Bounce Loop uses momentary rolls. Instant FX uses four GenErik DJ effects. REC uses the app's session recorder. [Reloop instruction manual](https://www.reloop.com/media/catalog/product/pdf/2/2/6/226018_Reloop_IM.pdf) · [Mixxx Beatpad mapping](https://github.com/mixxxdj/mixxx/blob/main/res/controllers/Reloop-Beatpad-scripts.js)

The jog ring pulses blue on the beat. The track-end warning uses the ring's red-plus-green setting. Check the resulting color on the controller. The original iCut crossfader gesture and the dedicated DIN cable's app compatibility remain open. The controller sets master, headphones and analog input gain in hardware.

## First iPad test

1. Power the Beatpad from its own supply.
2. Connect the rear USB port to the iPad through a Lightning to USB Camera Adapter.
3. Open GenErik DJ. In Settings → Controllers, check for the Beatpad controls and automatic split audio.
4. Load different tracks on the two decks. Play one on master. Cue the other in the headphones.
5. Check the jog touch, ring pulse and track-end warning.
6. If the Beatpad is missing, share the Controllers connection details and identify the cable in use.

Use the controller's own volume knobs for the listening check. This is the first physical test of the mapping and split audio.
