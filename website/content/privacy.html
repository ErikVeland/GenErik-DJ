How GenErik DJ uses your data.

Effective 18 November 2026. This notice covers the current GenErik DJ development app and this website. Erik Veland is the developer and privacy contact: erik@veland.au.

Data on your device

The app reads audio and music-library information to browse, analyse and play tracks. Local data can include titles, artists, album information, artwork, file references, audio fingerprints, waveforms, BPM, keys, beat grids, sections, cues, playlists and play history.

Recordings, their track information and any venue label you enter are stored with your recording data. Preferences, purchase-access state, custom names and logos are stored on the device.

Analysis runs on the device. The app’s bundled analysis models do not send music to an external AI service.

Permissions

Music-library permission lets the app read local songs and playlists. File selection grants access to the files you choose. Microphone permission supports microphone or line input from connected audio equipment.

You can refuse or revoke a permission. The related feature may then be unavailable. Revoking permission does not remove data that was already prepared or recorded.

Private iCloud preparation

When iCloud is available, the app can automatically share analysis and sections through your private CloudKit database while the app is open and decks are idle. Manual sync also saves preparation versions for recovery.

Cloud records include track metadata, audio identity hashes, analysis, waveform data and section information. Saved preparation can also include beat grids and cues. The audio files themselves are not uploaded by this preparation sync.

Apple provides iCloud storage and account services. The app keeps local copies of transferred records and an account-specific identifier to separate accounts. There is no separate GenErik DJ sign-up.

Stopping a transfer does not delete existing cloud records or permanently disable automatic sync. See privacy choices.

Purchases

The development app integrates Apple StoreKit for purchase and entitlement checks. Apple handles payment and Apple Account information. The app receives transaction and entitlement information needed to determine feature access. It does not receive your payment-card details.

Public purchase availability and pricing have not been announced.

Analytics and diagnostics

The audited app source contains no advertising SDK, behavioural analytics service or third-party automatic crash-upload service. It writes local diagnostic information for playback, library preparation and sync. Logs can include track names, file references, device state and error details.

Apple may process app diagnostics according to your device’s analytics settings and Apple’s policies. If you send logs or a screenshot to support, Erik receives the information you choose to include.

Services and sharing

Apple frameworks provide media access, audio, iCloud and purchase services. Bundled open-source audio and analysis components run locally. Their notices are on the credits page.

Using an export or share action sends the selected material to your chosen destination. That service’s privacy terms apply. The app does not contain advertising tracking or a data-sale integration.

This website

This website is hosted on the Glasscode server. It uses no advertising trackers, analytics scripts, cookies, remote fonts or third-party embeds.

The server processes your IP address and request information to deliver pages. Routine site access logging is disabled. Server error logs can contain request and connection details. Nginx error logs rotate daily and retain 14 rotations.

Contact links open your email application. Sending an email shares your address, message and attachments with Erik and the email services involved. The site has no contact-form database.

Retention and deletion

Local preparation remains until removed or the app’s local data is deleted. Exported recordings and backups can remain outside the app. Private iCloud records are separate from the app installation and are not removed merely by deleting the app.

The current sync implementation retains revisions for recovery and does not impose an automatic expiry. Contact Erik for help with cloud-data removal. Deletion requests for support correspondence are handled through the same contact address. Records may need to remain where the law requires them.

Your choices and requests

You can control system permissions, stop current transfers, remove local data and choose whether to send support information. Read the privacy choices page for instructions and limits.

To request access, correction or deletion of personal information held by Erik, email erik@veland.au. Describe the information involved. Do not send a password or identity document with your initial request.

Children

The app has no separate registration process that asks for age. A parent or guardian can contact Erik about information a child has sent to support.

Changes to this notice

This page will be updated when the app’s data handling changes. The effective date identifies the current notice. The final App Store release must have matching privacy declarations.
