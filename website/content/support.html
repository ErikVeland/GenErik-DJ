Common problems

A track will not load

Check that the audio is downloaded to the device and is not copy-protected. Subscription-protected tracks cannot be mixed. Check the app’s media permission in Settings on iPad or iPhone, or System Settings on Mac.

The beat grid is wrong

Check the grid against the waveform and the music. Use the track’s grid correction controls before relying on Sync. See track analysis.

No sound or headphone cue

Check the device volume, deck playback, crossfader and output route. Open Sound & Headphones to inspect routing. Disconnecting an audio device can change the active route.

Preparation has not synced

Check your iCloud account and network connection. Keep the app open with playback stopped. In Sharing & backup, select Sync now. Local preparation remains available if sync fails.

Audio breaks up or controls stall

This is a defect. Stop using the build for performance and report it. Include the device, build, connected equipment and the action that triggered it.

Report a bug

Email the following details:

  • Device model, operating-system version and app build number.
  • What you did, what you expected and what happened.
  • Whether you can repeat the problem.
  • Connected audio equipment and controllers, if relevant.

A screenshot or short recording can help. Remove personal information before sharing. Do not send music files unless requested and you have permission to share them.

Request a feature

Describe the mixing task you want to complete and where the current controls interrupt it. Send the request to Erik Veland.
