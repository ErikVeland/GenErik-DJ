Permissions, storage and deletion.

Revoke system permissions

  1. Open Settings on your iPad.
  2. Open Privacy & Security.
  3. Find Media & Apple Music or Microphone.
  4. Disable GenErik DJ’s access where listed.

Setting names can vary by iPadOS version. Removing permission does not erase prepared data or recordings.

Analytics and diagnostics

There is no optional third-party analytics switch in the audited app because no such analytics service is integrated.

Apple’s device analytics choices are under Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements. Review the options shown on your device.

Local diagnostic files remain on the device unless you share them or a device backup includes them. Review logs and screenshots before sending them to support.

iCloud sharing

In Sharing & backup, Stop sync stops the current transfer. Automatic preparation sharing can restart while the app is open and idle. This is not a permanent opt-out.

Review GenErik DJ’s iCloud access under your Apple Account settings, where available. The current app has no permanent in-app sharing switch or complete cloud-deletion control.

Cloud preparation is held in your private Apple account database. Deleting the app does not delete those records. For help removing them, email erik@veland.au. Do not send your Apple Account password.

Remove local app data

  1. Export recordings and preparation that you want to keep.
  2. Stop playback and close GenErik DJ.
  3. Open Settings, General, then iPad Storage.
  4. Select GenErik DJ.
  5. Select Delete App to remove the app and its local data.

Offload App keeps documents and data. Deleting the app does not remove exports, device backups or private iCloud preparation. Cloud data can return after reinstallation when sync resumes.
